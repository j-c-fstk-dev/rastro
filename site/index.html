Rastro

Every action leaves a trace.

Rastro records real-world impact: cleanups, planting, beekeeping, agroforestry, community work. Each action is signed by the people who did it and confirmed by the people who were there. No accounts, no wallets, no central server.

Open the app Run a community node

How it works

  1. Record

    Before and after photos, the place and what changed. It works without signal and sends itself when you are back online.

  2. Show the presence QR

    Everyone who was there scans it and confirms with their own key. People new to Rastro join in ten seconds, right there.

  3. The trail fills up

    Two witnesses make an action Confirmed. Five make it Consolidated. Nobody approves anything: the evidence adds up.

Built for the field, not for gatekeepers

No sign-up, no wallet
A key is created on the phone. No email, no password, no crypto to buy.
Works without signal
Records wait on the device and go out on their own when there is a connection.
No one holds the off switch
Records are copied across independent servers run by communities, over the open Nostr network. No single company or government can take them down.
Private by default
Public location is rounded to about 5 km and hidden photo data is removed before anything leaves the phone.

Proof you can hand to a sponsor

Groups keep a public list of members and see all their actions in one place. Any period becomes a printable impact report with totals, a map, photos and a verification link for every action.

  • Totals count only actions confirmed by witnesses
  • Export in the DeCleanup Network format
  • Free, open source, no subscription required

Keep your community’s records at home

An old laptop or a mini PC is enough. One command starts a relay, a photo server and a Tor address. Nodes copy each other, so no single machine matters.

Node setup guide

$ git clone https://github.com/j-c-fstk-dev/rastro
$ cd rastro/node
$ ./start.sh

Truth is not voted

Rastro follows Aletheia: a record holds because it is signed and witnessed, not because an authority approved it or a majority agreed.
